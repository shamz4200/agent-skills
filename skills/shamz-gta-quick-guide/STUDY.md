# Reference study: GTA 6 quick-guide Short ("You're selling your loot to the WRONG guy")

Owner sent this on 22 Nov 2026 as the model for a **daily GTA 6 guides / tips / viral news** series on @shamz888.
Style name: **Shamz Quick Guide** (see `docs/STYLES.md`). Study only: never copy its script, footage, branding or
layout one-to-one. The reference file itself is third-party media: keep it out of git (frames are git-ignored).

## Measurements
- 9:16 vertical, 720x1280 (we deliver 1080x1920), 30 fps, **38 s**.
- **92 spoken words in 37 s = about 150 wpm**: fast, punchy, one idea per sentence.
- Hard cuts at about 2.9, 5.1, 12.2, 19.5 and 23.6 s, but **something changes on screen every 0.5 to 1 s**: a new
  caption word, a number popping, a row lighting up, a stamp. Never a static second.

## Script shape (the formula)
1. **Hook in under 3 s, second person, a mistake the viewer is making**: "Bro, you're selling your loot to the
   wrong guy." Then "No, seriously." (the pattern interrupt).
2. **One surprising number**: "GTA 6 has 7 fences. 7." Then break it down (5 buy stolen goods, 2 only buy cars).
3. **Name names, give specifics**: which three are bad ("they're robbing you, lowest rates in the game") and which
   two are best for what (narcotics; jewellery and marked bills), with locations.
4. **A twist / stake**: "That bag you're carrying? Zero. Literally nothing, until a fence takes it."
5. **The action step**: "Check the map first, hover a fence, and it shows you his rates."
6. **CTA tied to the topic**: "Save this before your next robbery!" (+ a "Follow for part 2" pill).

## Look
- **Background**: gameplay footage, darkened (about 40%) with a vignette so the overlays read.
- **Captions**: bold condensed white sans, all caps, 1 to 4 words at a time, lower-middle of the frame; the key word
  gets a **solid colour highlight box** (magenta for wrong/bad, cyan or teal for good/key nouns, red for danger),
  sometimes the box text is dark on the colour.
- **Motion graphics on top of the footage** (this is what makes it feel premium):
  - an **in-game phone message** at the top (contact avatar + name, chat bubbles, a blue reply bubble "bro that's
    robbery");
  - **giant numerals** ("7", "2") with the footage visible *inside* the numeral (a mask), and a small spaced label
    under it ("FENCES IN LEONIDA");
  - **counter + segmented progress bar** ("5 STOLEN GOODS" in cyan beside "2 CARS ONLY" in magenta);
  - a **data card** ("Fence rates": rows with avatar letter, name, category, a little trend line, a Low/Best tag)
    whose rows light up as they are named;
  - a **value card** ("$0 · Not sold yet") that gets a red strike and flips to "Cash · Cashed out at a fence";
  - a **stylised map** with pins and a hover tooltip showing rates;
  - an **app notification** for the CTA ("Save this before your next robbery") and a "Follow for part 2" pill.
- A small channel chip top-left on every shot (theirs reads with their own name; ours: **@shamz888**).

# Shamz Quick Guide: GTA 6 Shorts, 6 a day

Start with [`README.md`](README.md) (pictures, rules, what gets delivered), then follow the full batch guide in
[`bulk/README.md`](bulk/README.md).

## The non-negotiables (owner rules)
1. **6 Shorts a day**, one batch, sent together.
2. **Shamz = the original painted character** (`cast/shamz-gta-sheet.png`), **a brand-new outfit every video**.
3. **His eyes always exactly like `cast/shamz_eyes_reference.png`**, painted on every Shamz image by `bulk/tools/eyes.py`.
4. **Zoom in on every hand and count five fingers**; no brand logos, no garbled lettering. Regenerate failures.
5. **New images for every video**, never reused.
6. **Facts verified** against several sources, kept in the script's `[SRC]` lines, **never in descriptions**.
7. **Max SEO upload pack** for all three platforms, **#shamz always**.
8. **Matching file names**: `gta6-<slug>.mp4`, `gta6-<slug>.png`, `gta6-<slug>.md`.
9. **Check every video frame by frame** (1 fps board) and fix before sending.
10. **Never commit** the owner's voice sample, music or photos.

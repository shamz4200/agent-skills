# Shamz Full-Packaging Mirror Checklist

A $shamz skill, part of the Shamz agent-skills collection at github.com/shamz4200/agent-skills. The moment a video goes live, package it fully and mirror it, and verify every item live instead of assuming it posted.

Use it immediately after any upload, in the same session. Packaging is part of the upload task, not a follow-up.

## The checklist

- Add the video to its series playlist, in episode order.
- Post the pinned comment, then pin it.
- Post the Community post. Exactly one. No duplicates.
- Upload and verify captions.
- Set the thumbnails and titles, and start Test & Compare where the platform supports it.
- Mirror to Instagram with the same caption.
- Mirror to TikTok with the same caption.

## Verify live, never assume

- Check every item on the live surface: playlist placement, pinned comment pinned, Community post visible, captions attached, thumbnails set.
- A post that reported an error may still have gone live. Check the live surface before retrying, so you never publish a duplicate.
- Report 'live and fully packaged' only when upload AND every packaging item are done and verified live.

## Rules it follows

- Packaging begins the moment the upload completes. No other work interleaves between upload and packaging.
- One Community post per video, never two.
- The same caption goes to every mirror surface.
- Sign-in steps are per account and stay out of this skill: confirm the destination account before posting anything.
- The pre-publish half of this job — verifying every destination and its sign-in route before building anything — is the publish-preflight-check skill. This skill is the execution and verification checklist that follows it.

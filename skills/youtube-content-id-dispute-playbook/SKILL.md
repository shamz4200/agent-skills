# Shamz Content ID Dispute Playbook

A $shamz skill, part of the Shamz agent-skills collection at github.com/shamz4200/agent-skills. When a Content ID claim lands on original work, file the dispute correctly, verify it live, and track the clock.

Use it for a Content ID claim against original music or video. It is not for strikes, and it is not for content you do not own.

## Before you file

- Confirm the channel in the Studio switcher first. A dispute filed on the wrong channel is a wasted dispute.
- Confirm the work is original: your own music, your own video, no third-party material under claim.
- Have the exact approved dispute text ready. Do not improvise it in the form.

## File the dispute

- Open the claim and choose Dispute.
- File under 'Original content'. All content is always original; every dispute is filed under 'Original content', never 'Licence'.
- Enter the exact approved dispute text.
- Tick the original-creation checkbox.
- Submit, and sign it with <your name>.

## Verify live

- Check the claim status on the live surface: it must read 'Dispute under review' with an expiry date, normally about 30 days out.
- Restrictions are suspended while the dispute is under review. Note the expiry date and track the clock.
- If the status does not show under review, the filing did not land. Do not assume it went through.

## Decide publish or hold

- Decide, per video, whether it stays published while the dispute runs or is held.
- Record the decision, the claim (<claimant name>, <video ID>), and the expiry date in the run note.

## Rules it follows

- Dispute reason is always 'Original content', never 'Licence'.
- Confirm the channel before filing anything.
- Verify the review live; a submitted form is not proof.
- Placeholders only in this skill: <claimant name>, <video ID>, <your name>, <channel ID>. Never put a real claim, video, signer, or channel into a published skill.

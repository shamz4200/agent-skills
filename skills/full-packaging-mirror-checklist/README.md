# Shamz Full-Packaging Mirror Checklist

A $shamz skill, part of the Shamz agent-skills collection. The checklist that runs the moment a video goes live: full packaging, mirror to Instagram and TikTok, every item verified live.

## What it does

1. Playlists the video, posts and pins the comment, posts exactly one Community post.
2. Verifies captions, thumbnails, and titles on the live surface.
3. Mirrors the video to Instagram and TikTok with the same caption.
4. Re-checks everything live after publishing, before reporting it done.

## What you need

- The live video URL and its title, description, and thumbnail files.
- The caption text for the mirrors.
- Signed-in access to each destination, verified before you post.

## How a run works

1. The upload completes. Start packaging immediately, in the same session.
2. Work the checklist top to bottom: playlist, pinned comment, Community post, captions, thumbnails.
3. Mirror to Instagram, then TikTok, same caption.
4. Verify every item live. Only then report 'live and fully packaged'.

## Rules it follows

- Never report 'live' and treat packaging as implied.
- One Community post per video, never two.
- Check the live surface before retrying anything that reported an error.

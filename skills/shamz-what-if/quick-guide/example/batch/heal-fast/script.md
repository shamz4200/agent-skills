You're healing wrong in GTA 6. Waiting it out won't fill your health.
Your health only refills on its own up to the white line on the bar.

Food helps too. A pink heart heals you back to full. An orange heart gives you extra health above your max.
Sleep at your safehouse between jobs to heal up.
And want more health for good? Hit the gym.
